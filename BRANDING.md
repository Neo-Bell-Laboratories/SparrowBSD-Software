# SparrowBSD visual identity

The site follows three principles: simplicity, elegance, power. Its layout uses
clear navigation, restrained typography, generous space, and the supplied logo's
cream and burgundy palette. Release status and package availability remain factual.

The original user-supplied artwork is preserved at
site/assets/sparrowbsd-logo.png. The transparent cutout used by the site is
site/assets/sparrowbsd-logo-transparent.png (1536 by 1024, RGBA PNG).
The cutout was edited with the built-in imagegen tool. Alpha was checked directly:
1,125,942 pixels are fully transparent, including the background inside the
crescent; phone and desktop browser previews verified no rectangular matte.

Final edit prompt:

> BACKGROUND REMOVAL / TRANSPARENT CUTOUT ONLY. Extract the sharply defined cream and dark burgundy logo shapes from the reference. Delete ALL background pixels including black corners AND the gray tan red glow within the crescent and between the clouds. Those regions must be fully TRANSPARENT (alpha 0), not dark or beige. Isolated emblem floating on transparent empty canvas. Keep the flying sparrow, the crescent moon, the three cloud shapes, the red sun's pointed rays and solid dark burgundy solar disk. Preserve shape and palette. Crisp flat-color logo, no lighting, NO glow, NO shadows, NO gradients in the background, NO matte. The space inside the crescent is transparent everywhere except the bird, clouds and sun. Deliver PNG with a real alpha channel.
